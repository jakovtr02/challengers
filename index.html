    THE RULEBOOK

    How to Play

    Each round has one hidden answer. Use the theme and clues to solve it in five tries or fewer.

    1. Start: See the theme, a cryptic clue, and the answer length.
    2. Wrong guess 1: Unlock Clue 2.
    3. Wrong guess 2: Unlock Clue 3.
    4. Wrong guess 3: Reveal the first letter.
    5. Wrong guess 4: Reveal another letter.
    6. Try 5: Your final chance to solve.
    BASE POINTS

    Try 1: 100 · Try 2: 75 · Try 3: 50

    Try 4: 25 · Try 5: 10 · Failed: 0

    Consecutive solves earn a multiplier up to +50%.

    Punctuation, capitalization, and a leading "the" don't matter. Some words also accept alternative names.
