# Challengers
**Think fast. Guess smart.**

Challengers is a word-guessing game where knowledge, deduction, and a little intuition help you uncover hidden answers. Each wrong guess unlocks more help—but solving sooner earns more points.

## How to Play

1. Choose your game mode and difficulty.
2. Read the theme and first clue.
3. Use the letter slots to work out the answer’s length. Spaces remain visible in multi-word answers.
4. Type your guess and press **Enter** or select the submit button.
5. Solve the answer within **five tries**.

Capitalization, punctuation, accents, and a leading “the” don’t affect matching. Some answers also accept alternative names or spellings.

Empty or repeated guesses don’t cost a try.

## Game Modes

- **Quick Play:** Five random answers from across the themes. Keep playing even if you miss a word.
- **Theme Challenge:** Up to eight shuffled answers from your chosen theme. The selected difficulty may leave fewer answers available.
- **Endless:** Keep solving until you fail a word or complete every available answer at your selected difficulty.

Choose **Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**, or **Mixed**. Even easy answers begin with an indirect clue.

## Progressive Hints

| Stage | Help available |
|---|---|
| Before your first guess | Theme, Clue 1, and letter slots |
| After one wrong guess | Clue 2 |
| After two wrong guesses | Clue 3—the most direct clue |
| After three wrong guesses | The first letter |
| After four wrong guesses | One additional random letter |
| Fifth guess | Your final chance |

Hints unlock through wrong guesses, not by waiting. Earlier clues stay visible.

Your guess history also shows how many letters match the answer in the correct positions. This comparison ignores spaces and punctuation; it doesn’t identify which letters matched.

## Scoring

| Result | Base points |
|---|---:|
| Solved on try 1 | 100 |
| Solved on try 2 | 75 |
| Solved on try 3 | 50 |
| Solved on try 4 | 25 |
| Solved on try 5 | 10 |
| Failed | 0 |

### Streak Bonuses

Consecutive solves increase your points:

- First consecutive solve: base points.
- Second consecutive solve: **+10%**.
- Third consecutive solve: **+20%**.
- The bonus continues rising until **+50%** on your sixth consecutive solve.

The bonus stays capped at +50%. A failed word resets your streak. Points are rounded to the nearest whole number.

**Example:** Your third consecutive solve, guessed on try 2, earns **75 × 1.20 = 90 points**.

## Round and Session Results

After each round, you’ll see the answer, a short fact, and your points earned. Select **Next** to continue.

At the end of a session, you’ll see:

- Total score.
- Words solved.
- Average tries for solved answers.
- Your best streak during the session.
- A high-score banner if you beat your previous best for that mode.

Select **Share Result** to copy an emoji summary. If automatic copying isn’t available, you can copy the displayed result manually.

Choose **Play Again** for another challenge or **Back to menu** to change your settings.

## Controls and Records

- **Enter:** Submit your guess.
- **Tab:** Move between controls.
- **Music-note button:** Toggle sound.
- **How to Play:** Open the rulebook.

Your best scores, best streak, total words solved, games played, and sound preference are remembered in your current browser when saving is available. Refreshing returns you to the menu.

## Tips for Better Guesses

Use the theme and answer length together. Re-read all the clues when new information appears, and pay attention to spaces in multi-word answers.

Treat the position-match count as a constraint rather than a measure of how close your guess is in meaning. Protect your streak—but remember that every extra guess reduces the round’s base points.
